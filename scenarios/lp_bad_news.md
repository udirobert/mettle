# Bad News: Meridian Pension

## Stakes

Meridian is the anchor LP and has already committed $40M. A founding
partner is leaving, and the key-person clause gives Meridian the right to
suspend. Other LPs are watching Meridian.

## Counterpart

Tom Becker backed the fund early and is loyal, but he has to report to his
committee this week. He will forgive bad news. He will not forgive
spin or a late call.

## Known objections

- Why now, and who else knows.
- Whether the key-person clause is triggered.
- Coverage for the departing partner's board seats.

## Win condition

Tom leaves with the facts, a dated plan, and words he can repeat to his
committee, and does not suspend the commitment.
