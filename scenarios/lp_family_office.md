# LP Meeting: Castell Family Office

## Stakes

A $15M commitment from a single-family office. There is no committee: Priya
and the family principal decide, often in the room.

## Counterpart

Priya Raman is warm and relationship-first. She listens for consistency
and for whether the GP is someone the family can trust through a bad year.

## Known objections

- The fee step-up feels like a tax on loyalty.
- Liquidity timing needs to match family distributions.
- She will notice if the story has shifted since last year.

## Win condition

Priya commits in principle, with fees framed as tied to outcomes rather
than negotiated down.
